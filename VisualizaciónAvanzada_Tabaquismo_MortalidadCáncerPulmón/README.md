# Smoking and lung cancer mortality in Spain

**¿Está funcionando la lucha contra el tabaquismo en España?**

A public health analysis of how smoking and lung cancer mortality have changed in Spain, compared with France, Italy, Greece, the United Kingdom and Norway, and of what young people are consuming now.

**Research question:** is the fall in smoking in Spain translating into better lung cancer outcomes?

## Key findings

- Adult smoking in Spain (WHO estimates) fell from 34.7% to 27.6% between 2000 and 2024, a slightly smaller fall than the EU-27 median.
- Lung cancer mortality among men has fallen 40% since 1995, while among women it has multiplied by 2.8 since 1990 and is at its peak.
- Among adults more men than women smoke daily, but among students aged 14 to 18 the pattern reverses.
- 26.3% of students vaped in the last month, more than the 21% who smoked tobacco.

The work describes trends; it does not evaluate the effect of any specific policy.

## Data sources

- **Our World in Data:** lung cancer mortality by sex; WHO age-standardised adult smoking estimates
- **WHO:** smoking prevalence for all countries (used to place Spain within the EU-27)
- **Eurostat:** daily and current smokers, 2014 and 2019
- **INE:** daily smokers by sex, Spanish health surveys 2009–2023
- **ESTUDES / EDADES (Ministerio de Sanidad):** tobacco and e-cigarette use among students aged 14 to 18, and adult context, transcribed from published reports

## How to run it

```bash
pip install -r requirements.txt
jupyter notebook tabaquismo_cancer_pulmon_espana.ipynb
```

All data is loaded in the "Carga de los datos" section at the start of the notebook, from the `Datos/` folder, with a check that every file is present. Running all cells regenerates `figuras/` and `dashboard.html`.
